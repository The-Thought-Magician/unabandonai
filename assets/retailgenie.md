# Retail Genie Early Access

Thank you for joining the movement and taking Retail Genie on a test ride with us!

Retail Genie is built for Retail associates across Merchandising, eCommerce, Technology, Digital, and Executive Leadership. You could wear one of the hats as you log in and prompt the platform to gain real-time intelligence on your business.

In Early Access, our model is trained with **Beauty and Cosmetics** datasets as the tip of the spear. The current transaction focus of the platform is the **top to mid marketing funnel.**

This means you would be able to access the **Search, Browse to Add to Cart** portions of the Consumer’s path-to-purchase.

Prompt suggestions are found on the left sidebar to get you started. But we do not expect you to stay on the road for long 😊 Go off-road, kick the tires, duress the Retail Genie app, and give us your valuable feedback and suggestions.

Our mission is to make the lives of Retail associates easier by collating intelligence across sources and enabling faster/accurate decision-making. This would enable the Retailer to adapt to changing consumer demand with speed and agility.
