# Blog — unabandonai

## Time to level the field and make Retail thrive!

#### **Problem – Intelligence Gap  
  
**Born “Digital-native" is more than a head start—it's a moat. Companies like Amazon dominate Retail, because they leverage real-time data to make customer-centric decisions across every function, from operations to customer experience (CX).

The rest of Retail world has 20 years digital intelligence of customer-product behavior data. Interestingly that intelligence is applied for front-office functions like Marketing and CX for the most part. This created a huge **intelligence-gap** between front (CX/Marketing) and back office (Business Operations) functions.  
  
Retailers resorted to "band-aid fixes" with MarTech tools to fundamental challenges and that did not work beyond a point.

Holistic transformation leveraging customer-behavior intelligence is one of the biggest aspects of how Retail runs today, compared to Amazon and a few others who drive every decision through the lens of customer demand and behavior.  

  

### **UnAbandon AI: The Future of Retail**

Bridging the intelligence-gap and make Operations and CX support each other seamlessly with AI is the founding vision of UnAbandon AI. 

UnAbandon AI is an AI-native, retail platform built to solve these problems with a unique and impactful approach.

The mission is to drive abandonment down, conversion up and make supply meet demand with customer-centric AI Agents assisting retail associates with contextual intelligence.

 **UnAbandon AI has 3 Components:**

1.  Retail Genie: A real-time interactive assistant providing insights for smarter, faster decisions across core functions – like merchandising, replenishment, inventory
    
2.  Custom Dash: Role-specific views for Merchandising, Marketing, eCommerce, Supply Chain, and Executives
    
3.  AI Agent Suite: Agents taking actionable intelligence from the model and recommending or taking next best action to take. Sharing a couple of examples below -
    

-   MerchAssist: For assortment planning.
    
-   PriceRite: For dynamic pricing optimization.
    
-   DemandSync: For real-time inventory management.
